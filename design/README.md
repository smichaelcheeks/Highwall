# Design

This directory contains authoritative, out-of-world guidance for creating and
evaluating Highwall lore and stories. It explains the author's creative intent;
it does not describe what people inside the setting know or establish what is
objectively true there.

Design documents may:

- guide worldbuilding, characterization, plotting, and editorial review
- explain why a proposed creative choice fits or conflicts with Highwall's aims
- identify preferred patterns, constraints, and anti-patterns

Design documents may not:

- serve as evidence that an in-world fact is true
- create unstated people, events, institutions, customs, or causal histories
- override explicit canon or silently resolve a contradiction
- turn a thematic preference into a universal belief held by fictional people

When a principle depends on a setting fact, link to that fact's authoritative
canon page. If the canon page does not establish it, treat the supposed fact as
unresolved regardless of what the design principle would make convenient.

Active principles are maintained in [`principles.md`](principles.md). Changes
to active design guidance require explicit authorial authority and the normal
intake audit trail.
