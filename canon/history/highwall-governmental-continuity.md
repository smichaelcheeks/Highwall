# Continuity of Highwall Government

## Pre-collapse system

The basic Council-Arbiter system and the institution of a Council-recognized
ruling family predate the collapse of the Old Wall. The titles and functions of
Arbiter, First Steward, Speaker of the Council, and Chief Magistrate are
inherited from the pre-collapse government. The collapse did not create a
wholly new constitutional order.

## Succession after the Old Wall

After the Old Wall collapsed, the serving Arbiter resigned. The Council then
moved to remove the existing ruling family entirely, and the Salverns became
the recognized ruling family in the aftermath.

It is historically rare for the Arbitership to pass directly from parent to
child.

## Scope boundary

The detailed evolution of present institutions from their pre-collapse forms,
the broader history of the former ruling family and the Salverns, and further
Old Wall history are not established here.
