# Regional Imperial Structure

## Objective structure

Each clan, city-state, and [Highwall](../places/highwall.md) governs itself as
an independent sovereign polity in its internal affairs. Collectively, however,
the region functions as a tributary province of an overseas empire.

The empire expects the region to supply ships, food, soldiers, and other
obligations. No single polity independently fulfills these regional
obligations. Each contributes according to its geographic and economic
specialization.

From the imperial perspective, the [upriver clans](../places/upriver-highlands.md)
fall within Highwall's provincial administration. Highwall's primary
responsibility in this arrangement is facilitating specialized raw materials
from the upriver clans to the downstream regions, where those resources
contribute to fulfilling imperial obligations.

## Official regional position

Within the region, Highwall, the upriver clans, and the downstream city-states
recognize one another as independent sovereign polities. Their relationships
are conducted through diplomacy, trade, treaties, and longstanding agreements
between neighboring states.

Official terminology emphasizes trade relationships, cooperation, and shared
obligations rather than tribute or subordination. The difference between this
language and the objective imperial structure is part of the
[reader reveal](../../story/reveals/regional-imperial-relationship.md).
