# Regional Trade System

## Regional specialization

The major geographic regions around [Highwall](../places/highwall.md) have
distinct, interdependent economic roles:

- The [Upriver Highlands](../places/upriver-highlands.md) emphasize herding and
  regional resource extraction.
- The [Stormlands](../places/stormlands.md), the region's largest supplier of
  timber and ore, export raw materials to Highwall and the downstream
  city-states.
- [Forge](../places/forge.md) imports Stormlands raw materials and converts them
  into ships, finished metal goods, and metal blanks used for coin production.
- [Ledger](../places/ledger.md) provides banking, moneychanging, commercial
  trust, coin minting, precision measuring equipment, and classical-era optical
  instruments.
- [Delta](../places/delta.md) supplies agricultural surplus to the coastal
  regions, Highwall, and many upriver clans.
- Highwall specializes in transportation, engineering, and facilitating trade
  between the upriver and downriver regions.

## Highwall's role

Highwall is the primary interface through which trade between the inland
plateau and the downstream regions passes. It connects the upriver pastoral
clans with the downstream city-states without replacing the larger commercial
centers farther downstream.

## Currency exchange

Paper financial instruments are widely used within Ledger. Many upriver
pastoral clans instead prefer barter or metal coinage. Ledger's banking system
therefore maintains a substantial moneychanging presence at Highwall, where
coastal paper notes are exchanged for metal coinage acceptable to many upriver
clans.

## Imperial obligations

Regional specialization also structures the region's obligations to the
overseas empire. The empire expects ships, food, soldiers, and other
obligations, but no polity fulfills them independently. Each contributes
according to its geographic and economic specialization. Highwall facilitates
specialized raw materials moving from the upriver clans to downstream regions,
where they contribute to fulfilling those obligations. The political authority
behind this system is documented in the
[regional imperial structure](../government/regional-imperial-structure.md).
