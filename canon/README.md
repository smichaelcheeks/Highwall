# Canon

This directory contains the authoritative description of what is true in the Highwall setting. It does not contain plot sequencing, reader reveals, brainstorming, or unsupported inference.

## Domains

- [`characters/`](characters/README.md): people and other individual actors
- [`places/`](places/README.md): geography, settlements, sites, and regions
- [`institutions/`](institutions/README.md): persistent social and civic institutions
- [`organizations/`](organizations/README.md): named groups and coordinated bodies
- [`history/`](history/README.md): events, periods, and historical chronology
- [`government/`](government/README.md): political systems, offices, and administration
- [`religion/`](religion/README.md): faiths, practices, doctrines, and religious bodies
- [`economy/`](economy/README.md): resources, trade, labor, currency, and economic systems
- [`technology/`](technology/README.md): tools, techniques, infrastructure, and technical constraints
- [`culture/`](culture/README.md): customs, languages, arts, identities, and social practices
- [`law/`](law/README.md): laws, courts, enforcement, rights, and legal customs
- [`terminology/`](terminology/README.md): authoritative terms, names, and usage

Every content page here must use YAML front matter. See [`../references/front-matter.md`](../references/front-matter.md). If a fact is unresolved, link to its record in [`../development/`](../development/README.md) rather than supplying an answer.
