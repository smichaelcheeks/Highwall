# Delta

## Summary

Delta is the setting's primary agricultural region and supports a significant
naval presence. Its agricultural exports are documented in the
[regional trade system](../economy/regional-trade-system.md).

## Water and agriculture

Most of Delta's water comes from a river originating in the
[Stormlands](stormlands.md). Delta also depends on seasonal flood pulses
emerging from [Highwall's](highwall.md) canyon to replenish nutrients in its
farmland.
