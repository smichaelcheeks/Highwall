# Forge

## Summary

Forge is a downriver place on the coastal plain. In Highwall shorthand,
`downriver` means on the coastal plain rather than merely any point farther
along a river. Forge is downstream of the Stormlands in both the literal and
supply-chain senses, and upstream of Ledger and Delta in both senses.

Forge's role in smelting, manufacturing, and regional exchange is documented
in the [regional trade system](../economy/regional-trade-system.md).

## Open issues

Forge's more specific place type and position within the coastal plain are not
established by the current sources.
