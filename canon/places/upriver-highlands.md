# Upriver Highlands

## Summary

The Upriver Highlands comprise the plateau, surrounding highlands, and inland
basin beyond [Highwall](highwall.md). Upriver pastoral clans inhabit these
areas.

## Economy

The clans' economies emphasize herding and regional resource extraction. Their
place in the wider exchange network is documented in the
[regional trade system](../economy/regional-trade-system.md).
