# Stormlands

## Summary

The Stormlands occupy the western portion of the downstream coastal plain.

## Geography and resources

The region contains forests with exceptionally large trees suitable for
construction timber and shipbuilding. Its economic role and exports are
documented in the [regional trade system](../economy/regional-trade-system.md).

## Hydrologic relationships

The river that provides most of [Delta's](delta.md) water originates in the
Stormlands.
