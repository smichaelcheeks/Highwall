# What are the canonical names of the major regional rivers?

## Context

providing most of Delta's water are established, but their canonical names are
not supplied. Highwall's river has a dedicated
[name question](highwall-river-name.md).

## Why this matters

Stable names will support later geography, trade, and story documentation.

## Constraints

- Do not assume that the documented river references describe the same river.
- The Highwall river is intermittent and non-navigable by ships.
- Most of Delta's water comes from a river originating in the Stormlands.
