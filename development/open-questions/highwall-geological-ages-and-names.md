# What are the Highwall region's geological ages and additional geological names?

## Context

The relative sequence of the two sandstone formations, faulting, ancient lake
drainage, canyon excavation, and later erosion is established, but their
absolute geological ages are not. Whitestone and Redstone are canonical names;
no additional canonical geological names have been supplied.

## Why this matters

Absolute ages would anchor the geological chronology, while additional names
would provide stable terminology for later specialized documentation.

## Constraints

- Redstone predates Whitestone.
- The ancient fault formed after both sandstone formations were deposited and
  later became inactive.
- Catastrophic drainage of the ancient inland lake followed the fault and
  excavated essentially the entire modern Highwall canyon.
- Whitestone and Redstone are already canonical formation names.
