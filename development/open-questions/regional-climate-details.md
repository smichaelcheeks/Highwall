# Regional Climate Details

## Context

The region's broad climate, two seasonal moisture systems, climatic zones,
storm season, drought pattern, and Highwall Canyon microclimates are
established. Detailed meteorological values and mechanisms are not.

## Why this matters

These details may later constrain ecology, agriculture, travel, construction,
water planning, and scene-level weather without being necessary to the broad
regional model.

## Constraints

- The plateau is predominantly semi-arid and lies in the coastal escarpment's
  rain shadow.
- Winter atmospheric rivers from the western sea and late-summer tropical
  moisture are the two principal seasonal systems.
- The eastern mountains hold substantial winter snowpack, and the eastern
  plateau has the more dependable late-summer storm season.
- Highwall Canyon runs north-south; its floor is generally more sheltered than
  its rim and the surrounding plateau.
- The hidden basin's climate remains unresolved.

## Unresolved details

- Latitude and Köppen climate classification.
- Regional temperature averages, frost dates, and growing-season lengths.
- Frequency of severe winter storms.
- Frequency and strength of atmospheric rivers.
- Prevailing winds outside the established seasonal patterns.
- Exact geographic extent of the late-summer storm belt.
- Climatic characteristics of the hidden basin.
- Detailed seasonal and diurnal wind patterns within Highwall Canyon.
- Frequency and inland extent of marine fog.
