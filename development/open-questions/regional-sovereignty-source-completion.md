# What is the complete regional sovereignty statement?

## Context

The Regional Geography and Economy seed ends during a sentence stating that
each clan, city-state, and Highwall governs itself as an independent sovereign
polity "in its internal". The omitted completion could materially affect the
scope or qualification of that political claim.

## Why this matters

The incomplete text cannot safely establish the region's broad political
structure without guessing at the author's intended limit or qualification.

## Constraints

- The source classifies the statement as objective setting truth.
- No missing words may be supplied by reviewer inference.
- The completed statement must be preserved in a new submission or addendum
  before integration.

## Possible answers

None proposed.

## Affected pages

- Future regional government documentation

## Resolution

The author supplied the missing continuation in the
[transmission correction](../../intake/submissions/2026-08-04-regional-geography-economy-a01.md).
The completed statement establishes that each clan, city-state, and Highwall
governs itself as an independent sovereign polity in its internal affairs. Its
integration is recorded in the [correction review](../intake-reviews/2026-08-04-regional-geography-economy-a01-review.md).
