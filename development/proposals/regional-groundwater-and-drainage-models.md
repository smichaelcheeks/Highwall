# Candidate Regional Groundwater and Drainage Models

None of these ideas are established canon:

- groundwater modeled on real sandstone aquifers, with the Colorado Plateau as
  an out-of-world inspiration;
- Dryrun alternating between visible and subsurface flow;
- multiple groundwater systems in different sandstone units;
- groundwater movement through fractures, bedding planes, and porous sandstone;
- spring behavior varying with recharge;
- historical drainage changes associated with the ancient lake's collapse.
