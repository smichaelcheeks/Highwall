# Did the modern intermittent river or the ancient megaflood form Highwall's canyon?

## Claim A

- **Claim:** `CASE-2026-08-04-HIGHWALL-OVERVIEW-S01-C002` says an
  intermittent river formed the canyon and produces catastrophic flash floods
  after seasonal storms.
- **Source:** [Highwall Overview Seed Review, claim C002](../intake-reviews/2026-08-04-highwall-overview-s01-review.md)
  and its [immutable submission](../../intake/submissions/2026-08-04-highwall-overview-s01.md).
- **Context:** The overview review originally accepted the compound statement
  as established canon.

## Claim B

- **Claim:** `CASE-2026-08-05-GEOLOGY-GEOMORPHOLOGY-S01-C020`, `C021`, and
  `C023` say the ancient lake-drainage megaflood excavated essentially the
  entire canyon and the modern intermittent river inherited it.
- **Source:** [Geology and Geomorphology Seed Review, claims C020-C023](../intake-reviews/2026-08-05-geology-geomorphology-s01-review.md)
  and its [immutable submission](../../intake/submissions/2026-08-05-geology-geomorphology-s01.md).
- **Context:** The geology review corrected canon prose but recorded no
  supersession of the earlier cross-case claim.

## Nature of the conflict

The modern intermittent river cannot both have formed the canyon and have
inherited a canyon formed essentially in full by the ancient megaflood. The
current canon pages use the megaflood model, but the earlier accepted claim
remained unsuperseded until A01.

## Impact

The conflict affects canyon formation, the distinction between the ancient
megaflood and ordinary seasonal floods, future hydrology and history intake,
and claim-index discovery.

## Possible resolutions

Before the decision, either claim could have governed only through explicit
author authority. Recency, scientific plausibility, and current prose alone
could not silently select a winner.

## Decision required

Determine whether the original river-formation claim or the later megaflood
model is correct canon and record the supersession relationship explicitly.

## Resolution

Resolved by
[`CASE-2026-08-04-HIGHWALL-OVERVIEW-A01-C001`](../../intake/submissions/2026-08-04-highwall-overview-a01.md).
The author explicitly confirmed that the megaflood is correct canon and that
the earlier claim should be superseded. The ancient inland-lake drainage
megaflood formed Highwall's canyon; the modern intermittent river inherited
it. `CASE-2026-08-04-HIGHWALL-OVERVIEW-S01-C002` remains preserved as source
history but no longer governs canon.
