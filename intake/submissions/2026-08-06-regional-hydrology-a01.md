- **Decision:** An ancient lake once filled the entire inland basin. It
  collapsed millennia ago and carved Highwall's canyon in a relatively short
  time.
- **Authority basis:** `explicit`
- **Clarifies or supersedes:** Clarifies S01-C016 and

  basin-filling lake, not every later or smaller lake in the basin.

### CASE-2026-08-06-REGIONAL-HYDROLOGY-A01-C002 — Confirm the present saline lake

- **Decision:** A much smaller saline lake remains in the inland basin in the
  present day. It overflows roughly once per decade, and its overflow joins the
  regular drainage basin for Highwall's river.
- **Authority basis:** `explicit`
- **Clarifies or supersedes:** Clarifies S01-C016 and
  `CASE-2026-08-05-GEOLOGY-GEOMORPHOLOGY-S01-C016`; no claim is erased.
- **Context:** The smaller present lake is distinct from the ancient lake that
  filled the whole basin.
