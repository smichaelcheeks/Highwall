## Setting truths

The region contains several distinct geographic regions that have developed different economic specializations.

Highwall occupies the principal practical corridor between the inland plateau and the downstream lowlands.

The river passing through Highwall is intermittent and is not navigable by ships.

---

## Places

### Highwall

Highwall occupies a deep sandstone canyon that cuts through the inland plateau.

The canyon provides the principal practical route between the inland plateau and the downstream lowlands.

Highwall specializes in transportation, engineering, and facilitating trade between the upriver and downriver regions.

Highwall does not replace the larger commercial centers farther downstream. Instead, it serves as the primary interface through which trade between the uplands and the downstream regions passes.

### Upriver Highlands

Upriver pastoral clans inhabit the plateau, surrounding highlands, and the inland basin beyond Highwall.

Their economies emphasize herding and regional resource extraction.

### Stormlands

The Stormlands occupy the western portion of the downstream coastal plain.

The Stormlands are the region's largest supplier of timber and ore.

The Stormlands contain forests with exceptionally large trees suitable for construction timber and shipbuilding.

Raw materials extracted from the Stormlands are exported to Highwall and the downstream city-states.

### Forge

Forge specializes in smelting and manufacturing finished goods.

Products include ships, finished metal goods, and metal blanks used for coin production.

Forge imports raw materials from the Stormlands.

### Ledger

Ledger is not a single contiguous state.

It is a collection of commercial cities and ports.

Ledger specializes in banking, moneychanging, minting coins from metal blanks, commercial trust, precision measuring equipment, and classical-era optical instruments.

Paper financial instruments are widely used within Ledger.

Ledger maintains a significant moneychanging presence at Highwall, where coastal paper notes are exchanged for metal coinage acceptable to many upriver pastoral clans.

### Delta

Delta is the primary agricultural region of the setting.

It supplies food to the coastal regions, Highwall, and many upriver clans.

Delta also supports a significant naval presence.

Most of Delta's water comes from the river originating in the Stormlands.

Delta also depends on seasonal flood pulses emerging from Highwall's canyon to replenish nutrients within its farmland.

---

## Relationships and dependencies

Stormlands exports timber and ore.

Forge converts raw materials into finished products.

Ledger provides regional banking, moneychanging, minting, precision equipment, and optical instruments.

Delta supplies agricultural surplus.

Highwall serves as the primary interface between the inland plateau and the downstream regions.

Highwall connects the upriver pastoral clans with the downstream city-states.

---

## Culture, systems, and terminology

Many upriver pastoral clans prefer barter or metal coinage rather than paper financial instruments.

Ledger's banking system therefore maintains a substantial moneychanging presence at Highwall.
