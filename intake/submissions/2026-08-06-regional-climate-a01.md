- **Decision:** Blueleaf harvesting and reed cultivation are established
  regional activities. Their existence and relationship to storm season may
  remain on the established climate page. Detailed organism descriptions,
  biological identities, and inventory treatment remain working canon unless
  separately established.
- **Authority basis:** `explicit`
- **Clarifies or supersedes:** Clarifies
  `CASE-2026-08-06-REGIONAL-CLIMATE-S01-C018` and `C025` without superseding
  either claim.

  Blueleaf, specify cultivation practices, or promote the working organism
  inventory.

### CASE-2026-08-06-REGIONAL-CLIMATE-A01-C002 — Identify Salt Basin

- **Decision:** Salt Basin is another name for the established inland basin
  beyond Highwall where the clans live. It is not a separate basin.
- **Authority basis:** `explicit`
- **Clarifies or supersedes:** Clarifies
  `CASE-2026-08-06-REGIONAL-CLIMATE-S01-C012`.
- **Context:** This establishes only the identity relationship and adds no new
  geography, hydrology, ecology, or clan detail.
