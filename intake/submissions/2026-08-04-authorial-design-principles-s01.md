# Authorial Intent and Design Principles Proposal

## Author instruction

Propose an appropriate repository location for authorial intent and design
principles, keeping them separate from canon and story and preventing their use
as evidence that an in-world fact is true.

## Session mode

`exploration`

## Submitted information

- The story is built around competent but imperfect people making reasonable
  decisions with incomplete information.
- Conflicts arise primarily from competing priorities, incentives,
  interpretations, uncertainty, institutional constraints, and chance rather
  than clear heroes and villains.
- Highwall is fundamentally a civilization shaped by centuries of adapting to
  one extraordinary environmental challenge: recurring catastrophic flash
  floods in a narrow canyon.
- The city's institutions, culture, laws, religion, engineering, economy, and
  civic values should generally emerge from that environmental pressure rather
  than existing arbitrarily.
- Institutions are intended to be functional, well-designed, and usually
  staffed by capable people, while remaining susceptible to blind spots,
  over-optimization, rare events, and changing circumstances.
- The setting values stewardship, accumulated knowledge, continuity, and
  long-term thinking over individual exceptionalism.
- The world should reward careful observation and historical context rather
  than convenient coincidence.
