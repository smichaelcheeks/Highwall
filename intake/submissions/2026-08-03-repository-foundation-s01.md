# Highwall Repository Initialization

## Author instruction

Establish a clean, scalable repository structure for Highwall without inventing or adding setting lore. Treat long-term maintainability and safe human/AI collaboration as higher priorities than completeness.

## Submitted information

The repository is to become the canonical source of truth for the Highwall fantasy setting, serving as an encyclopedia, novel-writing reference, searchable knowledge base, contradiction-detection system, and safely maintainable AI-assisted repository.

The requested design principles were:

- Give each fact one authoritative location and link instead of duplicating it.
- Distinguish established canon, working canon, unresolved ideas, discarded ideas, story structure, historical truth, and in-world beliefs.
- Assume nothing is canon unless explicitly documented.
- Never invent lore, silently resolve contradictions, or guess to fill gaps.
- Separate objective world information from narrative information such as character knowledge and reader reveals.
- Keep brainstorming, questions, proposals, contradictions, decisions, and retired ideas outside canon.
- Use portable Markdown and relative links without depending on a particular wiki generator.
- Design canon pages for YAML front matter including title, type, status, canon level, aliases, tags, and related pages.

The requested top-level areas were `canon/`, `story/`, `development/`, `references/`, and `templates/`. Canon was to anticipate characters, places, institutions, history, government, religion, economy, technology, culture, law, and terminology.

Reusable templates were requested for characters, places, institutions, historical events, cultures, government offices, organizations, technologies, decision records, and open questions. A professional README, AI-focused contribution rules, and any additional repository standards needed for safe future work were also requested.
